About GAO JI FOOD (S) PTE LTD

Since its humble beginnings in 1954, Gao Ji Food (S) Pte Ltd has undergone a transformational journey from a zinc-and-mobile kiosk to become a renowned heritage brand that has garnered a strong following of customers from all walks of life. Gao Ji Food possesses a strong foothold in Singapore’s Food & Beverage industry with over 50 food stall chains and restaurants island-wide.

About HUASING CONSTRUCTION & TRADING PTE LTD

Huasing Construction & Trading Pte Ltd is incorporated in 1994. Huasing started out as a transportation company focusing on heavy material transportation. Over the years, Huasing have expanded to a company that supplies all types of equipment. Huasing Construction & Trading Pte Ltd is a one stop construction equipment provider to cater to all your project needs. Exclusive Dealer for Develon and Powerscreen in Singapore.

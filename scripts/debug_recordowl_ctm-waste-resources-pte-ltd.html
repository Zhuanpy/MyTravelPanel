About CTM WASTE RESOURCES PTE. LTD.

CTM WASTE RESOURCES PTE. LTD. was founded in 1979 by Mr & Mrs Tan Chye Thiam, and has grown to be one of Singapore's biggest environment and building maintenance companies. It is involved in the treatment and disposal of waste (including remediation activities) with recycling of metal waste and scrap. The company focuses on delivering tailored, innovative, and environmentally sustainable solutions for facility and environmental management needs, incorporating purpose-driven innovation. They have a Centre of Excellence to seek out and scale innovations in technology and processes, with a commitment to People, Performance, Progress, and Preservation. CTM has expanded its portfolio and partnerships over the years, including securing contracts and partnerships with various organizations and iconic landmarks in Singapore.

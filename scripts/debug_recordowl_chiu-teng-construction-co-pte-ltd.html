About CHIU TENG CONSTRUCTION CO. PTE. LTD.

Chiu Teng Construction Co Pte Ltd, headquartered at 3791 Jalan Bukit Merah #03-05 E-Centre @ Redhill, Singapore, is a prominent player in the construction industry, specializing in a wide array of services that cater to both public and private sector projects. With a strong commitment to quality and innovation, the company has established itself as a reliable contractor for various construction needs, including residential, commercial, and industrial developments. The firm offers a comprehensive suite of services, encompassing general contracting, project management, and construction consultancy. Chiu Teng Construction is particularly noted for its expertise in building infrastructure that meets the highest safety and environmental standards. Utilizing advanced construction technologies and methodologies, the company ensures efficient project delivery while maintaining a focus on sustainability. Their dedication to leveraging modern techniques, such as Building Information Modeling (BIM) and prefabrication, allows for enhanced collaboration and streamlined processes, ultimately leading to cost-effective solutions for clients. Chiu Teng Construction Co Pte Ltd serves a diverse range of industries, including real estate development, urban infrastructure, and civil engineering. Their portfolio showcases a variety of successful projects, reflecting their capacity to handle complex builds while adhering to tight schedules and budgets. With a team of skilled professionals and a customer-centric approach, Chiu Teng Construction continues to contribute significantly to Singapore's evolving skyline, making them a trusted name in the construction landscape.
